How to start

Give each car’s year, make, and model. Add trim or EPA ID, asking price, and mileage if known. Tell us about your driving, passengers, budget, parking, and charging access.

You can ask about one car, too. Follow-up questions remember this conversation.

What evidence can’t tell you

  • No live listing search or current local market value.
  • No mechanical inspection or title history.
  • Model recalls do not prove VIN inclusion or repair completion.
  • Costs are estimates, not lender, insurance, repair, or purchase guarantees.
