What it can do

One conversation, several kinds of comparison.

Ask about one vehicle or compare two to five. DriveWise remembers the active conversation, so you can change an assumption or investigate the leading candidate without starting over.

01 / COMPARE

Rank a shortlist for your life

Scores affordability, energy use, space, weather and drivetrain fit, safety, and environmental priorities while keeping hard limits separate.

02 / MATCH

Resolve missing configurations

Matches trim, fuel type, drivetrain, engine, and transmission hints. With no trim, it selects a representative configuration and reports confidence and alternatives.

03 / COST

Estimate monthly ownership

Calculates loan payment, interest, fuel or electricity, insurance, and maintenance using your inputs and clearly labeled assumptions.

04 / EFFICIENCY

Retrieve EPA vehicle data

Finds MPG or MPGe, fuel type, annual fuel cost, engine, transmission, drivetrain, range, charging details, and available space information.

05 / SAFETY

Review safety evidence

Retrieves available NHTSA crash-test ratings and bounded complaint information without treating reports as proven defects or a reliability score.

06 / RECALLS

Check model-level campaigns

Shows campaign numbers, affected components, consequences, and remedies while explaining that model results do not prove VIN inclusion or repair status.

How to use DriveWise

Start broad, then follow the evidence.

You do not need a perfect specification. Give what you know, state which limits are firm, and let the agent disclose what it had to infer.

1

Name the vehicles

Provide a year, make, and model for each candidate. Add trim, listing price, mileage, or drivetrain when available.

2

Describe your routine

Include annual miles, commute, passengers, cargo, parking, charging, weather, and roads.

3

Separate limits from preferences

Say “hard limit” for a budget that should disqualify a vehicle. Otherwise, priorities influence the score without automatically ruling anything out.

4

Ask follow-ups

Change the APR, make snow more important, inspect recalls, or request a cost estimate for the leading vehicle. The active session remembers context.

You can leave the trim blank.

DriveWise will choose a representative EPA configuration when it can do so safely. It shows the selected configuration, confidence, alternatives, and any range that could affect the conclusion.

“Compare a 2023 Mazda CX-5 and Subaru Forester for four passengers, street parking, heavy snow, potholes, and 12,000 miles a year. I don’t know the trims.”

Good details to include

  • Asking prices and mileage
  • Down payment, APR, and term
  • Annual miles and commute
  • Parking and charging access
  • Passengers, cargo, weather, and roads

What DriveWise cannot verify

  • Live inventory or current market value
  • Mechanical condition or title history
  • Tires, ground clearance, or repair history
  • Whether a recall applies to a VIN or was repaired
