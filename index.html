How to start

Give each car’s year, make, and model. Trim is optional: DriveWise can select and disclose a representative EPA configuration. Add asking price and mileage if known.

Describe city/highway driving, passengers, cargo, parking, charging, and conditions such as snow, ice, heavy rain, extreme heat or cold, potholes, and gravel.

You can ask about one car, too. Follow-up questions remember this conversation.

What evidence can’t tell you

  • No live listing search or current local market value.
  • No mechanical inspection or title history.
  • Model recalls do not prove VIN inclusion or repair completion.
  • No invented ground clearance, tire performance, reliability, or flood/off-road capability.
  • Costs are estimates, not lender, insurance, repair, or purchase guarantees.
